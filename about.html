About

Where the design instinct comes from.

I'm a Computer Science student in Cairo, working at the intersection of technology, design, and AI. Coming from a CS background means I design with technical feasibility already in the room — I can tell you why a layout is annoying to build before it becomes an engineer's problem.

Most of my design work doubles as freelance and consulting practice for local Egyptian businesses — cafes, and increasingly larger concepts like Catalyst — which means starting from a real constraint or audit finding, not a blank canvas. I use Claude as a working partner across research, UI generation, and implementation — not to skip the thinking, but to move through it faster: explore, prototype, iterate, build, improve.

Outside of UI/UX, I build things that move — a 2D platformer with a partner, character and enemy animation, projectile systems. I like problems that sit between an interface and a system underneath it.
